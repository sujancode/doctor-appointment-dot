# Maple Street Family Clinic: before a visit

New patients are welcome. Please arrive 15 minutes early to fill in a short form.

Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of any medicines you take.

To cancel or change an appointment, call at least 24 hours ahead. A visit missed without notice may be charged $40.

Children under 16 come with a parent or guardian.

Flu shots are given at any appointment, and walk-in flu shots are on Friday mornings from 9am to 11am.

In an emergency, such as chest pain, trouble breathing, or heavy bleeding, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. Do not wait for an appointment.
