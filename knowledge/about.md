# Maple Street Family Clinic: about the clinic

Maple Street Family Clinic is at 18 Maple Street, Suite 200, on the second floor above the pharmacy.

The clinic is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. It is closed on weekends and public holidays.

Free parking is behind the building, and the entrance has a ramp and a lift.

The doctors are Dr. Priya Shah and Dr. Daniel Okafor, both family doctors. They see adults and children of any age.

Appointments are 30 minutes. Same-day appointments are kept for urgent problems: call in the morning.

The clinic takes most major insurance plans, including Blue Cross, Aetna, Cigna and United Healthcare. A visit without insurance costs $120.

The clinic's phone line is answered by Maya, its front desk assistant. Prescriptions, test results and medical questions are handled by the nurses, who call back the same day.
